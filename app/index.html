What shielding means

  1. A normal wallet is a glass box. Anyone can see the balance and every payment.
  2. Shielding puts test coins into a shared pool. You receive a private note. The pool records the seal, not the amount.
  3. A private send gives that note to someone else's shielded address.
  4. Withdraw sends the test coins to a normal address. That part is public again.
  5. Notes stay in this browser, locked by a wallet signature. Export a backup.

The coins are play money. The cryptography has not been audited.
